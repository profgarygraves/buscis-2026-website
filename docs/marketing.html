About the program

Earn an Associate in Science degree in Marketing Management, or certificates in Marketing Management and Digital Marketing.

The Digital Marketing Certificate is fully online and many students finish it in about two semesters. Its 1-unit, 8-week skill courses in SEO, search marketing, social media, email, content, and analytics are offered every semester.
