Start here

New to Fullerton College or new to the division? These four steps get you from first class to finished certificate.

  1. Apply to Fullerton College

    Admission is free. Apply once through CCCApply and you can take classes in any program.

    How to apply →

  2. Take a Start Here course

    Each program lists one or two first courses. They count toward the degree and show you what the field is like.

    See first courses →

  3. Map your plan with a counselor

    Use the Business pathway's program maps and meet a career education counselor to plan your semesters.

    Meet a counselor →

  4. Apply for your award

    Certificates and degrees are not automatic. Apply in the term you finish your last course.

    Forms & applications →
